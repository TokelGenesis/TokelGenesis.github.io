COMMUNITY CONTINUATION

A new beginning that keeps everything

Tokel Genesis 是 TokelPlatform 的社群延續。原團隊的貢獻永遠保留在歷史中；所有舊錢包、代幣、餘額完全相容，鏈上資料一點都沒變。

Tokel Genesis is a community continuation of TokelPlatform. The original team's contributions remain in the history forever. All existing wallets, tokens and balances are fully compatible, and nothing on-chain has changed.

FOR EXISTING HOLDERS

Your TKL is still here

Same chain

The chain is producing blocks again, continuing right after the original blocks. Every past transaction is intact.

Same TKL

Nothing to convert, swap or claim. Your addresses, balances, tokens and NFTs are exactly as before.

Wallets still work

Your existing seed phrase and keys work as always. Only enter them in a wallet you trust; nobody will ever ask you for them.

Stay safe

Tokel Genesis will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys, and there is no "migration", "claim" or "airdrop". The only official places are this website, TokelGenesis on GitHub, and imperialtokel@gmail.com.

WHY TOKEL

Tokens and NFTs, native on-chain

No smart contracts needed

Tokens and NFTs are created by the chain itself (Komodo Antara modules): no contract code to write and no contract bugs to fear.

Tiny fees

No gas bidding wars. Issuing and sending cost a tiny amount of TKL, affordable for anyone.

Proof of work

Equihash mining with 60-second blocks, plus checkpoints and deep-reorg protection for a safer network.

Open source, maintained

Node, wallet, light-client library and explorer are all open source, with security fixes and dependency upgrades done.

FAQ

You might be wondering

Is Tokel Genesis the official TokelPlatform?

No. It is a community continuation: the same chain and the original code (with all authorship and history kept), with ongoing fixes and updates.

What do I need to do to keep my TKL?

Nothing. Your TKL, tokens and NFTs stay at your addresses. Be careful with any message asking you to "migrate" or "claim": those are scams.

Why did the chain stop?

After the original team stopped operating, nobody kept mining, so blocks stopped. Mining has resumed and the chain is producing blocks normally; no data changed.

Can I mine?

Yes. Tokel uses Equihash and can be mined with an ordinary computer's CPU. See "Run a node and mine".

Why doesn't tokel.io open?

It is the original team's domain and its server has stopped. The new home of Tokel Genesis is this site and TokelGenesis on GitHub.

How do I contact you?

Email imperialtokel@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub.
